## Project Background

The **Katarungang Pambarangay (KP)**, or Barangay Justice System, is the cornerstone of community-level dispute resolution in the Philippines. However, many barangays still rely on manual, paper-based ledgers to record blotters, track hearings, and issue summons. 

**Luponv2** was developed to bridge the digital divide for **Barangay Bula**. The system transforms traditional bureaucratic processes into a streamlined, high-fidelity digital workflow. It is designed not just for data entry, but as a comprehensive legal tool that ensures procedural accuracy, document integrity, and long-term compliance with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) standards.

---

## Introduction

**Luponv2** is a professional-grade justice management platform built on the **Laravel 12** ecosystem. It serves as the single source of truth for all Lupon proceedings within the barangay, providing the office with a secure, searchable, and automated environment for conflict resolution.

### Core Objectives:
1.  **Justice with Precision:** Automating the generation of KP Forms (Summons, Notices, Settlements) to eliminate manual errors and ensure legal compliance.
2.  **Operational Efficiency:** Reducing the time required to generate LTIA (Lupong Tagapamayapa Incentives Awards) reports from days to seconds.
3.  **Data Sovereignty:** Establishing a secure, local-first Digital Archive that preserves case histories against physical loss or damage.
4.  **Desktop Readiness:** Hardened for deployment as a standalone Windows application to ensure high performance and reliability even in low-connectivity environments.

---

## Key Features

- **🛡️ Digital Blotter:** Unified entry point for all complaints, tracking complainants, respondents, and case status in real-time.
- **📅 Hearing Management:** Advanced scheduling for Mediation, Conciliation, and Arbitration with automated hearing history tracking.
- **📄 Pro-Grade Document Engine:** A "What You See Is What You Get" (WYSIWYG) editor specifically tuned for official legal forms with high-fidelity print layouts.
- **📂 Digital Archives (Filing Cabinet):** Monthly report packaging and archive snapshots for long-term records management.
- **📊 Compliance Reporting:** Real-time generation of LTIA-compliant transmittals and performance metrics.

---

## Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Laravel 12 (PHP 8.2+)
- **Frontend:** Livewire 3 & Alpine.js (Stateful interactivity without the complexity of a SPA)
- **Styling:** Custom Vanilla CSS & Tailwind CSS (Precision control for legal document fidelity)
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Tooling:** Vite, PINT (Code Styling), and PHPUnit (Testing)

---

## Deployment Context

Luponv2 is currently transitioning into a **Standalone Desktop Executable (.exe)**. This transition involves hardening the system's "View Modes" (Editor, Reader, Print) and ensuring that navigation remains stable in a non-browser-history environment.
